02 / Awarded support

Undergraduate scholarship

NHK Spring (Thailand) Undergraduate Scholarship

2026 – 2027
NHK SPRING (THAILAND) CO., LTD. • Academic Scholarship Program (2026–2027)
30,000 THB / Year • Continuing award: 2026 & 2027 • Conferred · 25 Sep 2026 • 2-month summer internship

Corporate undergraduate engineering grant awarded by NHK Spring (Thailand) Co., Ltd. (global manufacturer of automotive suspension, precision springs, and precision industrial components). The grant supports high-performing engineering students with strong academic standing, university faculty character endorsement, and engineering study.

Competitive Merit Selection: Awarded to select Faculty of Engineering students maintaining a cumulative GPAX of ≥ 2.75, certified good conduct from faculty advisors, and dedicated focus in Computer Engineering.

University Disbursement: Continuing educational sponsorship of 30,000 THB per academic year for both 2026 and 2027, disbursed through Srinakharinwirot University.

Industrial Internship Engagement: Includes a dedicated 2-month summer engineering internship placement at NHK Spring (Thailand) during the Year 3 summer term, with corporate housing accommodation and living allowance provided.

Presentation Ceremony: Officially attended the formal scholarship award presentation ceremony on September 25, 2026 at NHK Training Village, hosted by NHK Spring (Thailand) executive management.
