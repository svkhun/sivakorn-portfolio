Top 30 Team Overall • Top 10 in Track 2 (Data Science) • FlowSense Baseline • TrustGraph

Product Concept, Engineering & Pitch: Conceived the team's core concept, developed the K-NEXUS · FlowSense & TrustGraph prototype for financial planning and recipient-risk review, and delivered the on-stage pitch at KBTG KAMPUS Hackathon, placing as a Top 30 Team overall and Top 10 in the Data Science Track. View pitching experience →

Two-Tier Graph Architecture: The implementation combines past-only R-GCN 16D embeddings with LightGBM/ONNX inference. Saved synthetic test average precision was higher for the no-GNN baseline: 0.99640 versus 0.99373.

Measured prototype: No tested FlowSense ML forecast met the acceptance criteria; the 30-day forecast did not beat the baseline. Uncertainty, clamp-rate, and cohort-evidence requirements were not met; longer recursive forecasts worsened.

Decision flow: The Application uses a history/scenario/abstention spending baseline with FlowSense ML disabled. Bills, reserves, simulated saving and review share that spending basis. TrustGraph explains recipient-risk signals and provides a review path before eligible transfers. Explore the case study →
