Credit Risk Scoring & Explainable AI

A research prototype that estimates probability of default, shows model reason codes, and lets a reviewer explore hypothetical changes to a credit decision. It is a demonstration of decision support, not a regulator-approved lending system.

View Source Code Back to Directory
“A risk score becomes more useful when a reviewer can inspect the drivers, question the result, and record why a decision was made.”
01 // Problem & Approach

Make credit-model decisions inspectable

The project explores an underwriting workflow in which tabular credit features feed a probability-of-default model. The interface pairs the score with feature explanations, example reason codes, an audit trail, and policy scenarios.

Weight-of-Evidence screening and a LightGBM model support the research pipeline. TreeSHAP explanations and counterfactual examples help a reviewer see which inputs contributed to an outcome and what a hypothetical alternative might look like.

03 // Use & Boundaries

A review aid with further validation ahead

The prototype includes scoring and explanation views, example adverse-action reasons, scenario testing, and an audit record. These features can help demonstrate a transparent review process.

Before real lending use, a lender would need independent data-quality checks, calibration and drift monitoring, subgroup and fair-lending analysis, legal review, operational controls, and regulatory sign-off. The prototype alone does not establish those outcomes.
