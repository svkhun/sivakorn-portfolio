01 // The Regulatory Impasse

Monotonicity vs. Predictive Non-Linearity

In wholesale and retail credit adjudication, financial institutions are bound by strict supervisory oversight (such as the Bank of Thailand and Basel Committee internal ratings-based frameworks). Machine learning models cannot function as black boxes:

1. Strict Monotonicity: If an applicant's debt-to-income ratio increases, their risk score must never paradoxically decrease. Unconstrained deep neural nets and trees violate this fundamental economic sanity rule.
2. Right to Adverse Action Notice: Under fair lending legislation, institutions are legally obligated to disclose the top factors driving a loan denial, alongside realistic steps the applicant can take to cure the deficiency.

03 // Supervisory Transparency • Feature Engineering

Monotonic Weight of Evidence (WoE)

Using OptBinning (Mixed-Integer Programming), all continuous features are binned such that bad rates monotonically decrease with risk scores. This satisfies both bank risk auditors and rating agencies while maintaining an Information Value (IV) > 0.35:
