01 // Team Project

Bring three care perspectives together

The team concept brings patient, caregiver and physician workflows into a shared healthcare experience. Its architecture explores how daily follow-up and role-specific context can support coordinated care.

The Top 24 placement describes the team result. The project repository and architecture document are maintained by teammate Panupong-xD and provide the source for reviewing the team prototype.
