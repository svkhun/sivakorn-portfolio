Enterprise Credit Risk Scoring & Explainable AI (XAI)

Regulatory Framework (ECOA / FCRA / SR 11-7)

In regulated banking, models cannot operate as black boxes. The Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA, 12 C.F.R. § 1002.9) and Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) mandate automated, mathematically defensible Adverse Action Notices containing top adverse factors for rejected credit applicants.

Local vs. Global Interpretability (XAI)

Distinguishes global tree importance from instance-level Shapley values (\(f(x) = E[f(x)] + \sum_{j=1}^M \phi_j\)). Integrates SHAP TreeExplainer to output exact marginal risk contributions for transparent credit underwriting.

Weight of Evidence (WoE) & IV

Discretizes continuous variables into monotonic risk bins (\(\text{WoE} = \ln(\text{Good}\% / \text{Bad}\%)\)). Enforces linear relationship with log-odds of default, automatically neutralizing outliers and capturing non-linear relationships.

Population Stability Index (PSI)

Monitors covariate shift across quarterly baseline vs. actual borrower distributions (\(\text{PSI} = \sum (A_i - E_i) \times \ln(A_i / E_i)\)). Enforces \(\text{PSI} < 0.10\) threshold to prevent uncontained feature drift.

Asymmetric Cost Utility Matrix

Underwriting optimization where False Negatives (uncontained write-off loss on principal, \(C_{FN} \approx \$10,000\)) cost \(\approx 8\times\) False Positives (forgone interest margin on rejected prime borrower, \(C_{FP} \approx \$1,200\)).

Aihack Thailand 2025: Technical Dossier & Architecture

01 | The Business Challenge & Problem Formulation

Objective & Delinquency Target Binary classification of Long Overdue Debtors (LOD, defined as delinquency exceeding 90+ days past the initial 12-month tenure) on enterprise consumer loan portfolios.
Dataset Scale & Topology ~40,000 anonymized real consumer credit records (32,524 train / 8,619 test split) comprising demographic indicators, transactional payment histories, and bureau delinquency flags.
Asymmetric Cost Utility Matrix & Risk-vs-Volume Trade-off Formulated the optimal operating threshold balancing portfolio underwriting volume against write-off default exposure. Solved the Question Sheet to mathematically maximize net portfolio interest yield while minimizing expected credit loss (ECL).

02 | Feature Engineering & Tabular Modeling Strategy

Delinquency Velocity Signals Engineered temporal repayment acceleration ratios, revolving credit limit exhaustion gradients, and tenure-adjusted missed installment counters across multi-period payment windows.
Imbalance Mitigation & WoE Calibrated severe 1:12 class default skew using focal cross-entropy loss weights, stratified splits, and monotonic Weight of Evidence (WoE) risk binning with Information Value (IV) filtering.
Algorithmic Ensembling & Optuna Evaluated GBDT architectures using 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation on tuned LightGBM, XGBoost, and CatBoost models. Hyperparameters tuned via Bayesian Optuna search.

03 | Production Validation & Submission Results

Blind Private Test Split Generalization Achieved ~0.894 ROC-AUC on the public leaderboard and retained top-tier performance on the blind private test split, verifying zero target leakage and resilient variance control.
Executive Committee Pitch & Defense Presented modeling methodology, P&L sensitivity curves, and adverse action interpretability directly to executive directors at Jaiyossompati Building 3, Chulalongkorn Business School.

04 | Official Credentials & Verification

Organizing Consortium AIRA & AIFUL Public Company Limited, Chulalongkorn Business School (CBS), and ProbSpace.
